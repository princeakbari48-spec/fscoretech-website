PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Explore the capabilities

Features and settings may vary by platform and software version. Contact FSCoreTech to confirm the current release.

CALLING

Calls and contacts

✦

Dial pad

Enter numbers with an easy-to-use keypad, recent-call shortcuts, and contact navigation.

  • Number entry with a dedicated keypad
  • Access recent calls and contacts
  • Call-focused controls for quick navigation
✦

Incoming and active calls

Answer or decline calls, view caller information and call duration, and use mute, speaker, and keypad controls.

  • Caller information and call status
  • Answer and decline actions
  • Mute, speaker and in-call keypad controls
✦

Call activity

Review daily and date-range call metrics, including answered and missed calls, talk time, and usage amounts.

  • Daily and date-range call summaries
  • Answered and missed call counts
  • Talk-time and usage calculations
TRANSCRIPTION

Speech and call workflow

✦

Multilingual transcript

English, Dari/Farsi, and Pashto transcription options with language-appropriate text direction.

  • English transcription options
  • Dari/Farsi and Pashto language modes
  • Right-to-left text for supported languages
✦

In-call transcript view

A dedicated transcript area with live and stop controls, designed for use during calls.

  • Dedicated transcript reading space
  • Live and stop transcription controls
  • Designed for an active call workflow
✦

Cloud-directed connectivity

Provider-connected calls, rather than local-device pairing, support the communication workflow.

  • Provider-connected communications
  • Designed without local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth pairing
  • Available services depend on provider configuration
