Privacy Policy

Effective: October 18, 2026

This policy covers the public FS Core Tech website at fscoretech.com. It does not describe the data practices of individual applications or separate services.

Information this website collects

If you submit an application access request or enquiry, we collect your full name, email address, phone number, address, selected application, and the details you provide. Do not include passwords, payment card numbers, or other secrets. We use these details to review and respond to your request.

Email contact

Website requests are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile and sent through Brevo's email delivery service to contact@fscoretech.com, which is hosted by Zoho Mail. The visitor's validated email address is used as the reply-to address. Cloudflare processes information needed to provide security verification; Brevo processes message content and delivery data; and Zoho processes the delivered email. Each provider's own privacy terms also apply to its processing.

Hosting and technical logs

Cloudflare may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser details, timestamps, and request information for hosting, security verification, reliability, and delivery. These providers may retain information under their own terms and settings.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts. Turnstile is loaded when the request form is used to protect the endpoint from automated abuse.

Retention and your choices

We retain request messages in our email service for as long as needed to review, respond to, and keep records of the request. Provider backups or security logs may have separate retention periods. To ask us to correct or delete a request, email contact@fscoretech.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the website or project changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to fsp@fscoretech.com.
